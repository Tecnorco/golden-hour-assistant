# Answer every inquiry in minutes, without giving up control

*A one-page overview for small service businesses (photographers, cleaners, tutors, trades).*

## The problem

You started your business to do the work, not to write emails. Yet every week you answer the same questions:
*How much would that be? Are you free on the 14th? What happens if we cancel?* Each reply means looking up
prices, checking your calendar, doing the math, and writing it nicely. Reply too slowly and the customer books
someone else.

## What the assistant does

It reads each new inquiry and prepares a reply for you to check:

- **Quote requests:** it works out the package, checks your calendar, adds weekend or travel fees exactly as
  your price list says, and lays out an itemized quote with the deposit.
- **Missing details:** if the customer forgot the date or location, it politely asks for everything missing
  in one email, instead of guessing.
- **Policy questions:** it answers from *your* written policies (cancellations, delivery times, what to wear)
  and nothing else.
- **Reschedules and cancellations:** it sends a warm acknowledgement and flags the request for you to handle.
- **Spam and scams:** it filters them out, including emails that try to trick an AI into giving discounts.

Every draft is written in your voice: your greeting, your sign-off, your tone.

## You stay in control

- **Nothing is sent without your click.** You approve, edit or reject every reply on one simple screen.
- **Prices come from your price list, never from the AI's imagination.** The math is done by a calculator, not
  a language model, and every amount is checked before you see it.
- **Every policy statement shows where it came from**, so you can check it in seconds.
- **When it is unsure, it says so** and puts the email in your queue instead of improvising.

## Does it work?

On a test set of 20 realistic emails for a photography studio, it routed every email correctly, calculated
all 8 quotes exactly right, blocked both scam attempts, and sent nothing without approval. A separate AI review
step found no unsupported claims in any draft. Running costs are about **one cent per email** in AI usage.

These are results on a small test set. A pilot with your real emails is how we find out how it performs for
your business, and you approve every reply throughout.
