# What to Wear and What to Expect

A little preparation goes a long way. Here is our guide to looking and feeling your best on camera.

## What to wear

**Choose a colour palette, not a uniform.** For families and groups, pick two or three colours that go well together (for example navy, cream and rust) and let everyone mix and match. Matching white shirts and jeans can look dated.

**Solid colours and soft textures photograph best.** Knits, linen, denim and flowing dresses add depth. Avoid large logos, neon colours and very small busy patterns, which can create a strange shimmer in photos.

**Dress for the season and the location.** For outdoor sessions in fall and winter, layers like scarves and jackets look great and keep you comfortable. Bring comfortable shoes for walking to the location, and change into nicer ones for the photos.

**Kids:** pick outfits they can move in. Bring a backup outfit and wipes. Happy kids make better photos than perfect outfits.

**Bring options.** For a Standard Portrait Session (90 minutes) you can do up to two outfit changes. For a Mini Portrait Session (45 minutes) we recommend one outfit.

## Hair and makeup

Do your hair and makeup the way you normally like it, maybe just a little more polished. Avoid trying a brand-new look on the day. A matte powder helps with shine. Professional makeup is welcome but never required.

## What to expect on the day

- **Arrive 10 minutes early.** Sessions start on time so that the light is right.
- **Golden hour.** Outdoor sessions are usually scheduled in the hour after sunrise or before sunset, when the light is soft and warm. That is where our name comes from!
- **Guided posing.** You do not need to know how to pose. Maya will guide you into natural poses and give you small prompts, like walking together or telling each other a story.
- **Breaks are fine.** Especially with kids, we take breaks whenever needed.

## Events and weddings

For events, please send a rough schedule and a list of must-have shots (for example, the cake cutting or a group photo with the team) at least one week before. For weddings, we schedule a planning call about one month before the date to go through the timeline, family groups and venue details.

## Pets

Pets are welcome in outdoor portrait sessions! Please bring a helper to hold the leash and some treats.

## Feeling nervous?

Almost everyone does. Most people tell us they forgot about the camera after the first ten minutes. We keep things relaxed and fun.
