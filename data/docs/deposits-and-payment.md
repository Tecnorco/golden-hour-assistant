# Deposits and Payment

## Deposit to book

A **30% deposit** of the total quoted price is required to book any session, event or wedding. Your date is not reserved until the deposit has been received and Maya has sent you a written booking confirmation. We do not hold dates without a deposit, even for a few days, because our calendar fills quickly.

The deposit is **non-refundable** (see the cancellation and rescheduling policy for the details and for the rebooking credit).

## Paying the balance

- **Portrait sessions:** the remaining balance is due on the day of the session, before we start.
- **Events:** the remaining balance is due **7 days** before the event.
- **Weddings:** the remaining balance is due **30 days** before the wedding date. Couples can split the balance into up to three monthly payments, as long as the last payment arrives 30 days before the wedding.

Add-ons booked after the deposit (for example, adding a printed album later) are simply added to your balance.

## Accepted payment methods

We accept debit and credit cards, bank transfer and Zelle. We send a secure payment link with every invoice. **We will never ask you for card details by email or text.** If anyone does, please let us know.

Cash is accepted for portrait session balances on the day.

## Price quotes

Every quote is itemized: the package, any add-ons, any weekend surcharge, any travel fee, and the total and deposit. Quotes are valid for **30 days**. Prices are set in our price list and are the same for everyone. We do not offer discounts by email negotiation, but we do run occasional seasonal mini-session events, which are announced on our website and newsletter.

## Weekend surcharge

Saturday and Sunday sessions carry a **10% surcharge on the package price**. Add-ons and travel are not affected by the surcharge.

## Travel fees

Travel within **25 miles** of our studio in Willowbrook is free. Beyond 25 miles, travel is charged at **$1.50 per mile** for each additional mile (one way). If your location is not on our usual list, we will ask for the town or venue address so we can calculate the distance correctly.

## Late payments

If a balance is more than 7 days late, we may hold delivery of your gallery until it is paid. We will always remind you first.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are available in any amount and are valid for one year. They can be applied to any package or add-on.
