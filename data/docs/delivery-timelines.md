# Delivery Timelines

One of the most common questions we hear is "when will I get my photos?". Here is exactly what to expect.

## Portrait sessions

Mini and Standard Portrait Sessions are delivered within **2 weeks** (14 calendar days) of your session date.

You will receive a private online gallery link by email. From the gallery you can download full-resolution files, share with family, and order prints. We usually post 3 to 5 "sneak peek" photos within 48 hours so you have something to share right away.

## Event coverage

Event galleries (parties, corporate events, galas, quinceañeras and similar) are also delivered within **2 weeks** of the event. Corporate clients who need a handful of images for same-week press or social media can ask for a selection of up to 15 images within 72 hours at no charge; just mention it when you book.

## Weddings

Wedding galleries are delivered within **6 weeks** of the wedding date. Weddings involve many more images and a careful editing pass for colour and consistency across the whole day, so they take longer.

Wedding couples get a sneak peek of 20 to 30 images within one week.

Printed albums are designed after you choose your favourite images. Album design takes about 2 weeks after you send your selections, and printing and shipping take another 3 to 4 weeks.

## Rush delivery

Need your photos sooner? Our **rush delivery add-on ($150)** gets your full edited gallery to you **within 5 days** of the session. Rush delivery is available for portrait sessions and events. For weddings, rush delivery covers a set of 100 highlight images within 5 days, with the full gallery still following within 6 weeks.

Rush delivery must be booked at least 48 hours before the session so we can plan our editing schedule.

## Busy season note

From September to December we are at our busiest. Our timelines above still apply, but we kindly ask for patience with email response times during those months.

## How long galleries stay online

Online galleries stay active for **12 months**. Please download and back up your images. After 12 months, we keep an archive copy for another year and can reactivate a gallery for a small fee.

## What "edited" means

Every delivered photo is individually colour-corrected and lightly retouched (skin smoothing, stray hairs, blemishes that are not permanent). Heavy retouching such as body reshaping or removing people from a scene is not included.
