# Weather and Backup Plans

We photograph outdoors a lot, so weather is part of the job. Here is how we handle it.

## Portrait sessions

Maya checks the forecast **48 hours** before every outdoor portrait session. If rain, strong wind or extreme temperatures are likely, she will contact you to choose one of these options:

1. **Move indoors.** Our Willowbrook studio has a natural-light room that works well for families of up to six people.
2. **Reschedule for free.** Weather reschedules do not count as your one free reschedule, and there is no fee.
3. **Go for it.** Light overcast is actually great for photos, and a few people love rainy-day photos with umbrellas.

If the weather turns during the session, we pause and wait, move to a covered spot, or finish the remaining time on a new date. You never pay twice.

## Events

For events, the venue is usually indoors or has a covered area. If an outdoor event is moved indoors at the last minute, we adapt; there is no extra charge. If an event is cancelled because of severe weather (for example, a storm warning issued by authorities), we treat it as a cancellation by us and you can reschedule for free or receive a full refund.

## Weddings

Your wedding happens rain or shine, and so does our coverage. During the planning call one month before the wedding, we will:

- walk through a **rain plan** for each outdoor part of the day (ceremony, portraits, group photos);
- identify covered spots at or near your venue;
- suggest a buffer of 15 to 20 minutes in the timeline for weather surprises.

We carry clear umbrellas for couples and lighting equipment to make indoor portraits look just as good as outdoor ones.

## Extreme heat and cold

In summer we schedule outdoor sessions near sunrise or sunset to avoid the worst heat. In winter, mini sessions outdoors are kept short and we recommend warm layers (see our what to wear guide).

## Safety first

We will never ask you, your guests or your kids to stand somewhere unsafe for a photo, including near water in strong wind, on icy paths or during lightning. If Maya decides conditions are unsafe, the session or that part of the day moves indoors or is rescheduled.

## What weather does not change

Weather does not change your price or your package. Your deposit is never lost because of weather.
