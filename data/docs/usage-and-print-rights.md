# Usage and Print Rights

This page explains what you can and cannot do with your photos, and what we may do with them.

## Who owns the photos

Under copyright law, Golden Hour Photography (Maya) owns the copyright of all images we create. When you book with us you receive a **personal use license**, which covers almost everything people want to do with their photos.

## Your personal use license

You may:

- Print your photos at any size, at any print lab you like.
- Share your photos on social media, websites and in messages. A tag or credit (@goldenhourphoto) is appreciated but not required.
- Use photos in personal projects such as holiday cards, family albums, wedding thank-you cards and invitations.
- Give copies to family and friends for their personal use.

You may not:

- Sell the photos or use them in paid advertising without a commercial license.
- Enter the photos into photography contests as your own work.
- Heavily edit or apply filters and then credit Golden Hour Photography. Light cropping is fine.

## Commercial use

Business clients (headshots, corporate events, products) often need commercial rights. Corporate event coverage includes a **commercial license** for the company that booked us, for marketing, press and internal use. Other commercial uses can be added on request; Maya quotes these individually.

## Prints from us

You can order professional prints directly from your online gallery. Prints ordered through the gallery are produced by a professional lab and come with a colour guarantee. Our **printed album** add-on ($300) is a 10x10 inch lay-flat album with 20 spreads.

## How we use your photos

We love sharing our work. We may use a small selection of images in our portfolio, on our website and on social media. If you would prefer we do not share your photos, just tell us before your session. Privacy requests are always honoured, no questions asked, and they do not change your price.

We never share photos of children publicly without written permission from a parent or guardian.

## Raw files

We do not provide unedited raw files. The edited images are the finished product, and the editing is part of our style.

## Lost photos

If you lose your files after your gallery expires, contact us. We keep archive copies for two years after the session date and can usually help.
