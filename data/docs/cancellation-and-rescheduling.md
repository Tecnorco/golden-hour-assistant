# Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

Life happens, and we get it. This policy explains what happens if you need to move or cancel your session. It applies to every package: portraits, events and weddings.

## Rescheduling a portrait session

You can reschedule a Mini or Standard Portrait Session **once, free of charge**, as long as you let us know at least **72 hours** before your session start time. Your deposit moves with you to the new date.

If you need to reschedule with less than 72 hours notice, we will still do our best to find a new date, but a **$50 late-change fee** applies. A second reschedule of the same session is treated as a cancellation and a new booking.

New dates depend on availability. Maya confirms every new date personally; nothing is final until you get a confirmation email from her.

## Rescheduling an event or wedding

Events and weddings take a lot more planning on our side, so the rules are a little different:

- Moving your date **more than 90 days** before the original date: free, one time, subject to availability.
- Moving your date **within 90 days** of the original date: your deposit transfers, but a **$200 rescheduling fee** applies.
- If we cannot offer the new date you want, we treat the request as a cancellation (see below).

## Cancellations by you

Your **30% deposit is non-refundable** because it holds a date that we turn other clients away for. Beyond the deposit:

- Portraits cancelled with at least 72 hours notice: no further charges.
- Portraits cancelled with less than 72 hours notice, or no-shows: the full session fee is due.
- Events and weddings cancelled more than 60 days before the date: no further charges beyond the deposit.
- Events and weddings cancelled within 60 days: 50% of the remaining balance is due.

If you cancel and later rebook within 12 months, we will credit **half of your forfeited deposit** toward the new booking. This is a goodwill credit, not a refund.

## Cancellations by us

If Maya is ill or has an emergency, we will first try to send a trusted associate photographer (with your approval). If that is not possible, you get a **full refund**, including your deposit, or a free reschedule, whichever you prefer.

## Weather

Outdoor sessions affected by weather are covered by our separate weather and backup plans policy. Weather reschedules never count as your one free reschedule.

## How to request a change

Reply to your booking email or write to the studio with your name, your current date and the new dates you would like. Please give two or three options. Maya will check the calendar and confirm in writing, usually within one business day.
